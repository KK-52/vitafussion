ℹ️ About VitaFusion AI

Architecture, datasets, methodology, and system overview

🧬 Project Overview

VitaFusion AI is a multimodal healthcare intelligence platform designed for early health-risk assessment using four distinct data modalities: retinal (fundus) images, tongue images, ECG signals, and structured clinical data. By integrating multiple health indicators through a cross-modal attention fusion architecture, the system provides comprehensive risk predictions for cardiovascular disease, diabetes, hypertension, arrhythmia, and metabolic syndrome.

The platform incorporates Explainable AI (Grad-CAM, SHAP, Integrated Gradients) for model transparency, and Large Language Model integration for generating clinically meaningful, personalized health reports.

🧠 Model Architecture Details

👁️ Retinal Encoder

BackboneResNet-50 (ImageNet V2)
Output512-dim embedding
TasksDR Grading, Vessel Density, Macular Risk
XAIGrad-CAM (layer4)
ReferenceHe et al. (2016) "Deep Residual Learning"

👅 Tongue Encoder

BackboneEfficientNet-B0 (ImageNet V1)
Output512-dim embedding
Tasks6-class tongue classification, Coating, Moisture
XAIGrad-CAM (last conv block)
ReferenceTan & Le (2019) "EfficientNet"

📈 ECG Encoder

ArchitectureMulti-Scale 1D-CNN + BiLSTM
Output256-dim embedding
Tasks8-class arrhythmia, HR, PR, QRS, QTc
AttentionTemporal self-attention pooling
ReferenceHannun et al. (2019), Wagner et al. (2020)

🧾 Clinical Encoder

Architecture3-Layer MLP + Residual + LayerNorm
Input22 clinical features
Output256-dim embedding
Risk ModelsFramingham, ADA, JNC-inspired
XAISHAP feature attribution
